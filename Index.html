🚨 Cardiac Arrest – What to do NOW

Step 1 – Recognise (10 sec)

  • Responsive? "શું સાંભળો છો?"
  • Breathing? છાતી ઉપડે છે?
  • Carotid pulse 10 sec?
  • ના → CODE BLUE + CPR શરૂ

Step 2 – CPR

  • 100–120/min, depth 5–6 cm, full recoil
  • Ratio 30:2 (Ambu bag)
  • દર 2 min compressor બદલો

Step 3 – Defibrillator

  • VF/Pulseless VT → તરત shock 120–200 J
  • દર 2 min rhythm check

Step 4 – Drugs (doctor order)

  • Adrenaline 1 mg IV every 3–5 min
  • Amiodarone 300 mg IV after 3rd shock
5H/5T: Hypoxia, Hypovolemia, Hyper/Hypokalemia, Hypothermia, H⁺ | Tension pneumothorax, Tamponade, Toxins, Thrombosis

🧠 GCS Calculator

Eye (1–4) | Verbal (1–5) | Motor (1–6)

  • Eye: 4 Spontaneous, 3 Command, 2 Pain, 1 None
  • Verbal: 5 Oriented, 4 Confused, 3 Inappropriate, 2 Sounds, 1 None
  • Motor: 6 Obeys, 5 Localizes, 4 Withdraws, 3 Flexion, 2 Extension, 1 None
GCS ≤8 → Doctor ને તરત બોલાવો (intubation જરૂરી)

  • Adrenaline, Noradrenaline
  • Insulin, Heparin
  • Potassium chloride (KCl)

📌 Dilution – 5 નિયમ

  • Ampoule = original; diluted નહીં
  • લેબલ વાંચો – નામ + dose + expiry
  • High-alert = double-check
  • Infusion pump જ
  • Label – drug, dose, time, prepared by

💧 IV Fluids & Drip Rate

Drops/min = (Volume in mL × Drop factor) / Time in minutes
દા.ત. 500 mL NS over 8 hr, factor 20: (500 × 20) / 480 = 21 drops/min
  • KCl – Never IV push; dilute + pump
  • Extravasation → તરત બંધ + doctor

🔴 Anaphylaxis

  • Red flag: urticaria + wheeze + BP drop
  • Adrenaline 0.3–0.5 mg IM (thigh)
  • O₂, IV NS 500–1000 mL

🟡 Hypoglycemia

  • GRBS <70 → conscious: oral glucose
  • Unconscious: D50 50 mL IV
  • 15 min પછી GRBS ફરી ચેક

🟠 Seizure

  • સલામત જગ્યાએ સૂવડાવો, મોંમાં કંઈ ન નાખો
  • Time નોંધો – 5 min થી વધુ = status

🐍 Snake Bite

  • Tourniquet ના બાંધવો
  • Limb immobilize કરો, Bite site કાપો નહીં
  • ASV (order) + monitor allergy

💉 Needle-Stick

  • સાબુ-પાણી, લોહી દબાવો નહીં
  • તરત report + PEP (2 hr માં)
